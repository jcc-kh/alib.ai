# National Gallery DC 2012 (NGDC)

## The case
About 10 days, with two intertwined plots: an art theft and a defacement, centred on a gallery employee. Main people: **Carry** and **Tracy**. Plans are discussed across their devices. The scenario ends with a report to police, devices seized and network logs requested.

**Getting at the phone's databases is extra work.** The phone copies are raw disk images, not folders of files. The messages, calls and contacts are inside `blk0_mmcblk0.bin` and need a forensic tool (Autopsy, The Sleuth Kit) or a loop mount to pull out. The database layouts will be Android 2.3 or 4.0 era, so today's parsers may need adjusting. We have not done this.

## Answer key (read)
`answers.zip` contains:
- `NGDC __ Instructor Packet.pdf`: the whole scenario. Read this first.
- A final evidence report for each of: Carry's phone, Carry's tablet, Tracy's iPhone, Tracy's MacBook Air, Tracy's external hard drive, the emails, the network logs.

## Emails (read)
`email.zip` holds 13 `.eml` files and a README. They are **keylogger logs from Tracy's MacBook Air, emailed every hour to `joe.sum.twelve@gmail.com`**. Each file has standard headers (From `root@Tracys-MacBook-Air.local`, To, Date, server route, sending IP) and the captured keystrokes as the body.

## Fit for Alib.ai
- **Good:** several people with several devices each; six copies of one phone over time, so deletions can be seen between copies; a full answer key with a report per device; small downloads.
- **Weak:** the phone copies are raw disk images that need a forensic tool to open; 2012 devices, so current parsers may not fit; we have not seen any message data from it yet.
