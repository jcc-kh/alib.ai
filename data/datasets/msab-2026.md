# MSAB 2026 Digital Summit CTF

Built by Hexordia for MSAB, the company behind the XRY forensic tool. It is not a Cellebrite dataset.

## The case
| Device | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 12 | **Blaise Suttoni** | Employee at "Chromatic Confections". Looking for another job |
| Samsung S20 | **Emily Min** | Blaise's boss. Has a Samsung secure folder, an alternate Instagram account (`easy_e_money96`), a second account (`galactic.gateaux`), a password manager and a "SECRET plan" |

Other people: **Dorian** (Blaise apologises to him and calls him) and **Layla** from Glassdoor (a job offer). A MacBook is mentioned, but no copy of it is published.

The two phones are one storyline: Blaise sends Emily a "Rainbow Funnel Cakes" recipe on Signal, and Emily's phone shows her asking Blaise for baked goods.

| `https://msab26.hexordiactf.com/SamsungS20.zip` | 39 GB | `SamsungS20FFS.zip` (9 GB), `FFS.xry` (23.8 GB), `RAM.xry` (3.3 GB), `Data.bin` (12.9 GB), `EncryptionKeys.json` |

No password. The server supports range requests.

- **iPhone:** fetch individual databases with `scripts/fetch_from_remote_zip.py` (about 100 MB).
- **Samsung:** `SamsungS20FFS.zip` is compressed inside the outer zip, so it can't be read in place. Download that one 9 GB entry and skip the rest.
